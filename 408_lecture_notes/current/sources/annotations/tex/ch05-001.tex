{\color{ink}\bfseries 例 5-1}\par\vspace{1bp}每个非根结点恰对应一条入边，因此\(\sum i\cdot n_{i}=\allowbreak{}n-1\)；减去\(\sum n_{i}=\allowbreak{}n\)，得\(-n_{0}+\allowbreak{}\sum_{i\ge2}(i-1)n_{i}=\allowbreak{}-1\)。本题\(n_{0}=\allowbreak{}1+\allowbreak{}3\times 20+\allowbreak{}2\times 10+\allowbreak{}1\times 1=\allowbreak{}82\)，选 B。
