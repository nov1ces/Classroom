{\color{ink}\bfseries 例 5-5}\par\vspace{1bp}每次合并使参与子树中全部叶的码长增加1，该步新增WPL等于新权。合并过程为\(3+\allowbreak{}4=\allowbreak{}7\)，\(5+\allowbreak{}6=\allowbreak{}11\)，\(7+\allowbreak{}8=\allowbreak{}15\)，\(10+\allowbreak{}11=\allowbreak{}21\)，\(15+\allowbreak{}21=\allowbreak{}36\)；\(\mathrm{WPL}=\allowbreak{}7+\allowbreak{}11+\allowbreak{}15+\allowbreak{}21+\allowbreak{}36=\allowbreak{}90\)，加权平均码长\(90/\allowbreak{}(3+\allowbreak{}4+\allowbreak{}5+\allowbreak{}6+\allowbreak{}8+\allowbreak{}10)=\allowbreak{}2.5 bit/\allowbreak{}\)字符，选 B。
